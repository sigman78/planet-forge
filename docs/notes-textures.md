Notes:
- Sources were the 2K-JPG zips, downsampled to 1024 with LANCZOS. Normals use NormalGL only and are renormalized after resize.
- Height is percentile-normalized (0.2 to 99.8) to full range. ORM: AO from the AmbientOcclusion map, roughness, metalness 0 (none of these sets has metalness).
- `meanAlbedo` is linear 0..1, computed from the saved JPEG. `tileMeters` is set by hand in the table (ambientCG dimension data is 0 for most of these sets).
- water `tileMeters` of 12 is a guess; tune it in the renderer.
- Caveats: beach_sand and desert are both warm tan (desert is brighter and more orange); the renderer may want a tint. Rock normals are strong and may need a lower normal scale. Forest is blobby at large scale, which suits far LOD.
- Total size is about 21 MB.
